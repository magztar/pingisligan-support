Pingisligan

Bring your table tennis group together. Keep track of matches, results and standings.

Help and privacy information from Magnus Larsson, developer of Pingisligan.

Need a hand?

Email magztar@icloud.com. Describe what happened and include your app version, iPhone or iPad model, and iOS version if possible. Never send passwords or sign-in codes.

Common questions

How do I share a room?

Sign in to iCloud on your device. The room owner invites participants using the app's iCloud invitation. Participants need Pingisligan and accept the invitation link on their devices. The owner chooses read or write access.

How do I send result emails?

The room owner opens Settings → Result emails, saves the recipient list and chooses a report. Sending requires a configured account in Apple's Mail app. Check the report, recipients and sender account before sending.

How do I unsubscribe from result emails?

Use the unsubscribe link in a result email to send a request to the room owner. The owner then disables your address manually. Your membership and match results remain.

How do I delete my information?

Open Account and privacy → Delete my app data. Your own rooms are deleted and you leave shared rooms. Information entered by other room owners must be handled by them. Read the privacy policy for details.

Which languages are supported?

English, Swedish, German, Danish, Norwegian Bokmål and Finnish. The app follows your device's language preferences.
