Table Tennis League

Bring your table tennis group together. Keep track of matches, results and standings.

Available on the App Store as Pingisligan:TableTennis League. The icon label is Pingisligan; the welcome screen uses the equivalent name in your language. Developed by Magnus Larsson.

From a quick match to a club session

Keep score for singles and doubles, or play Americano with rotating opponents and partners. Save results, follow individual standings and manage your players in a private club.

Large score cards work on iPhone and iPad. Tap to add a point, or briefly hold a card to select or correct a score. Choose the first server and receiver or use the automatic draw. The scoreboard follows service order and changes sides between games, and at five points in the deciding game in the 11-point format.

A dedicated TV scoreboard shows up to four ongoing club matches at a time and rotates when more are running. It includes table numbers, standings, a club banner and winner celebrations. Americano celebrates the individual winners after the session.

Common questions

How do I get started?

Sign in to iCloud in your device settings, open the app and enter a display name. Create a club or accept an iCloud invitation. On Play, add or select players, choose a format and table, and start scoring. When you reopen the app, it returns to Play in your saved club after the launch screen.

Can several matches run at once?

Yes. Select a table for each session. All players included in an active session are reserved, including players resting in an Americano round. An unfinished session with no activity for more than an hour is automatically removed and its players become available again. Internet access is needed to keep devices in sync.

How do I rename or delete a club?

The owner can change the club name in Club. Deleting players, matches, a season or the club requires owner mode with Face ID, Touch ID or the device passcode. Check carefully before confirming deletion.

How do I show matches on a TV?

Connect one device to an external display and use the TV scoreboard. That device can show ongoing matches from the same club while other devices keep score. Keep the devices connected to iCloud and the internet.

How do I share a club?

Sign in to iCloud on your device. The club owner invites participants using the app's iCloud invitation. Participants need Pingisligan and accept the invitation link on their devices. The owner chooses read or write access.

How do I send result emails?

The club owner opens Club → Result emails, saves the recipient list and chooses a session, a date or the season for the report. Sending requires a configured account in Apple's Mail app. Check the report, recipients and sender account before sending.

How do I unsubscribe from result emails?

Use the unsubscribe link in a result email to send a request to the club owner. The owner then disables your address manually. Your membership and match results remain.

How do I delete my information?

Open Account and privacy → Delete my app data. Your own clubs are deleted and you leave shared clubs. Information entered by other club owners must be handled by them. Read the privacy policy for details.

Which languages are supported?

English, Swedish, German, Danish, Norwegian Bokmål and Finnish. The app follows your device's language preferences.

Americano and the recreational 21-point format are alternatives to standard table tennis scoring. No ITTF certification or affiliation is claimed.
